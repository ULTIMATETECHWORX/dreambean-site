Features

Everything a bedtime story needs

Create, listen, play, and revisit adventures shaped around your child's imagination.

🌱

Dream Garden

Choose a dreamer, explore a dreamworld, and add a surprise. Review the ingredients, then grow them into a four-chapter adventure.

🫙

Dream Jar

Share a wish, a feeling, and a gentle hope. DreamBean keeps that meaning at the heart of a warm, personalized story.

✨

Surprise Me

Let DreamBean choose the ingredients for a playful new adventure when inspiration needs a little nudge.

How it works

A story in four simple steps

A guided journey from one small idea to a story your family can read, hear, and play with.

1

Choose your path

Pick the Dream Garden, Dream Jar, or Surprise Me to begin your story adventure.

2

Add your ideas

Type in a hero name, pick a magical setting, or share a wish — it's your story.

3

Watch it come alive

Grow the tale one chapter at a time, make a prediction, and choose an art style when you are ready to illustrate it.

4

Listen, play, and revisit

Hear the story in a favorite voice, save it to the Library, or open the Playroom for story-based games.

Age Groups

Stories that grow with your child

DreamBean adapts story complexity, vocabulary, and illustrations based on your child's age group.

🌙
Ages 5 – 8

Explorer

Simple, warm, and magical stories told in the present tense with gentle vocabulary and bold, colorful illustrations.

  • Short sentences and simple words
  • Present tense narration
  • Bold, friendly illustrations
  • 60 – 80 words per chapter
⚡
Ages 9 – 12

Creator

Richer adventures with more complex plots, atmospheric illustrations, and expanded vocabulary to challenge growing readers.

  • Richer vocabulary and descriptive language
  • Past tense narrative style
  • Atmospheric, detailed illustrations
  • 100 – 140 words per chapter

Child Safety

Built safe from the ground up

Every layer of DreamBean is designed to keep young imaginations in a safe, positive space.

🛡️

AI Content Moderation

Every text input is screened before reaching the story engine. Inappropriate content is blocked instantly with a child-friendly message.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent-Controlled Accounts

Accounts are created by parents or guardians. Age groups are protected by a math challenge gate so only adults can change them.

🔍

Story Safety Review

Every AI-generated story is reviewed by a second AI layer before delivery to ensure age-appropriate content and positive themes.

🚫

No Ads. Ever.

DreamBean contains zero advertisements. Children's attention and data are never sold to advertisers.

🔒

Minimal Data Collection

We limit collection to what the service needs, such as parent account details, story content, subscription status, and technical service data. We do not use story content to profile children.

✅

COPPA Compliant

DreamBean is designed with COPPA requirements in mind. Accounts and protected settings belong to a parent or legal guardian.
