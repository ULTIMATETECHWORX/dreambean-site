How does Dreamavera use AI?

AI helps write chapters from your family's ideas, generate story illustrations, and turn chapter text into narration. Separate safety checks help review text and images. Story creation and new narration require an internet connection; this website does not generate stories.

Can we use Dreamavera in English or Spanish?

Yes. The app interface, new stories, and narration support English and Latin American Spanish. Choose English or Español (Latinoamérica) on the sign-in screen or in Settings → Language. Existing stories and their next chapters keep their original language; changing the setting does not translate saved stories. The included showcases contain three English stories and one Spanish story. This website is currently in English.

Can we try it before subscribing?

Yes. New users can create one free Dream Garden story and one free Dream Jar story. The app also includes four finished showcase adventures with illustrations and narration: three in English and one in Spanish.

Is it for independent readers or listening together?

Both. Explorer stories are designed for ages 5–8, and Creator stories for ages 9–12. Narration and sentence highlighting support listening along. Children should use a parent- or guardian-owned account with adult supervision.

What are the four story games?

Story Order, Memory Match, Dream Detective, and Picture Puzzle revisit a completed adventure. The games use the story's existing text and illustrations rather than creating a separate game account.

Where can I check what my subscription includes?

Open Subscription → Plan details in the app before purchasing. Each plan includes monthly creation allowances for stories, illustrations, and narration. Replaying included showcases, saved recordings, and story games does not use generation allowance.

What is saved or synced?

Your Library saves stories on your device. Premium can also sync story content and illustrations across devices. Generated narration is cached locally and is not included in cloud sync. Read more about your data.
